Terminal Site

The BlueOcean LNG terminal site is located about four kilometers south of Stewart, BC. The 270-hectare property has approximately 1.8 kilometers of deep-water shoreline, providing excellent access for LNG carriers.

Stewart’s community and infrastructure—including an airport, medical clinic, port facilities, and power lines—support industrial development.
